I am an independent creative based in Munich, interested in photography, architecture, visual culture and design.

I find inspiration in the ordinary: the geometry of a building, the character of a photograph, the atmosphere of a place and the stories hidden in everyday life.

This journal is a collection of observations, experiments and creative explorations.
